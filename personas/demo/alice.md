## Backstory
Alice is a project manager at a mid-size consultancy downtown. Every morning she
drops her seven-year-old at school before heading to the office. She bought her
car specifically because the northern transit links are unreliable before 8 AM.
She's heard about the new congestion charge but hasn't looked into the details —
she assumes it won't be that bad.
